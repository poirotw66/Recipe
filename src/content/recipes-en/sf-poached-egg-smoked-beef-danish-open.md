steps:
  - Slice 2 Danish pastries crosswise; butter the cut sides and bake at 180°C /
    350°F 5–7 minutes until golden and lightly crisp.
  - Rest 160 g smoked beef from the fridge until lightly softened, keeping
    slices intact; squeeze mustard to taste into a small bowl and set aside.
  - Bring a saucepan of water to a boil with 1 tbsp vinegar; reduce to low, stir
    a whirlpool with a spoon, crack in 2 eggs, and poach about 3 minutes; drain
    well.
  - Brush 1 tbsp olive oil on toasted Danish; layer smoked beef slices and top
    with 2 poached eggs.
  - Sprinkle 1/3 tsp sea salt and 1/4 tsp cracked black pepper, dot with mustard
    to taste, and serve hot.
tips:
  - Prepare the fillings first and cook the eggs last. Cook eggs through, or use
    pasteurized eggs if serving them runny.
  - Use pasteurized eggs for runny or soft-cooked eggs; cook ordinary eggs until
    both white and yolk are set.
