intro: "Brown the sausage with the spices, then cook the clams and shrimp until the shells open and the shrimp are opaque. Remove the seafood, stir the saffron rice through the pan juices, and add the broth gradually."
steps:
  - "Prep: purge and rinse 400 g clams; peel 120 g shrimp, leaving the tails; thickly slice 140 g sausage; shred 40 g cheese; prepare 220 g saffron rice; and heat 200 ml broth."
  - "Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a skillet over medium heat. Cook the sausage for about 2 minutes, until some fat renders and the edges brown. Add 1/2 tsp chili flakes, 1/3 tsp sea salt, and 1/4 tsp cracked black pepper; stir to combine."
  - "Add the clams and shrimp, cover, and cook over medium heat for about 3–4 minutes. As each clam opens, remove it; discard any that remain closed. Cook the shrimp until opaque and a food thermometer inserted in the thickest part reads 63°C (145°F), then remove them. If they have not reached the target, keep cooking and check again. Keep the pan juices."
  - "Add 220 g saffron rice and stir it through the pan juices for about 1 minute."
  - "Add hot broth in half-ladle portions and stir through 3–4 rounds over about 6 minutes, allowing the rice to absorb it. The list gives 200 ml; the amount absorbed needs a kitchen test."
  - "Return the clams, shrimp, and sausage and toss over high heat for about 1 minute to combine; avoid leaving the seafood to cook for longer."
  - "Turn off the heat and fold in 40 g cheese until partly melted and distributed through the rice."
  - "Plate and serve hot."
tips:
  - "Use the listed 120 g shrimp. Purge and rinse the clams; remove the seafood when the shells have opened and the shrimp are opaque so it does not continue cooking while the rice is prepared."
  - "Keep the pan juices for the saffron rice. Heat the listed 200 ml broth and add it in half-ladle portions, letting the rice absorb each addition before adding more."
  - "Fold in the cheese after turning off the heat. Return the seafood near the end and combine as directed."
storage: "Serve soon after cooking. Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours and reheat to 165°F (74°C) before eating."
