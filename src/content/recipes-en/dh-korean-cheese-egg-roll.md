steps:
  - Beat 3 eggs with 1/4 tsp salt and 1 tsp toasted sesame oil in a mixing bowl.
    Cut 60 g mozzarella into short pieces, slice 1 cheddar slice into strips,
    and cut 30 g flying fish roe and 50 g crab sticks into small pieces.
  - Preheat a nonstick skillet or egg-roll pan over low heat and add the neutral oil and tilt the pan to coat it lightly. Pour in about one-third of the egg mixture and
    tilt to make a thin layer.
  - When the surface is still slightly wet and the edges begin to set, place
    about one-third of the mozzarella, cheddar, roe, and crab sticks along the
    side nearest you.
  - Roll gently with a spatula and push the roll to the far side. Pour in
    another third of the egg mixture, letting it run underneath to connect the
    new layer.
  - Repeat with the remaining egg and filling to make about three layers. Once
    the egg mixture is used, cook the roll gently on all sides until set.
  - Use a food thermometer to confirm the center reaches 71°C (160°F) and the
    egg is fully set and cheese melted. If needed, continue cooking over low
    heat and recheck.
  - Transfer to a clean cutting board, rest 1 minute, slice into about 2 cm
    rounds, and serve hot.
  - Optionally garnish with a little scallion.
tips:
  - Cook over low heat and roll while each egg layer is still slightly moist;
    add the next layer gradually so it seals to the roll.
  - Confirm the center reaches 71°C (160°F) and the egg is fully set before
    serving.
