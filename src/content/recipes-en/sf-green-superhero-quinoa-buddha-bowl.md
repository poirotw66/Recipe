intro: Cook the quinoa, roast the cauliflower and chickpeas until hot, then
  assemble the bowl with avocado and lemon dressing. This recipe makes no
  specific nutrition or weight-loss claims.
steps:
  - Rinse the quinoa in a fine-mesh strainer under cool running water for about
    30 seconds, then drain well. Add it to a saucepan with 120 ml water and
    bring to a boil. Cover, reduce to low, and simmer about 15 minutes. The
    water should be absorbed and the grains should lighten as their white germs
    uncurl into fine curls. Turn off the heat and let stand covered for 5
    minutes, then fluff with a fork. If the water is gone but the centers are
    still firm, add a small splash of water, cover, and continue simmering until
    tender.
  - Heat the oven to 200°C / 400°F. Cut the cauliflower into similar-size
    florets and drain and pat the cooked chickpeas dry. Toss with half the oil,
    salt, and pepper. Spread them on the baking sheet with a little space
    between pieces. Roast for 18–22 minutes, turning once halfway through, until
    a fork slides easily into the cauliflower centers, the edges are lightly
    golden, and the chickpeas are hot throughout.
  - Slice the avocado and halve the tomatoes. Stir the lemon juice with the
    remaining oil.
  - Spread the quinoa in the bottom of a bowl, add the roasted cauliflower and
    chickpeas, then top with the avocado and tomatoes. Spoon over the lemon
    dressing just before serving so the warm and fresh components keep their
    separate textures.
tips:
  - Cook the quinoa and toppings separately and assemble just before serving.
    A crowded pan can make the vegetables release moisture instead of browning.
    Larger cauliflower pieces may need extra roasting; cook until tender through
    the center.
storage: Refrigerate cooked quinoa, cauliflower, and chickpeas separately within 2 hours and
  use within 3 days. Reheat until steaming; cut avocado and tomatoes just before serving.
