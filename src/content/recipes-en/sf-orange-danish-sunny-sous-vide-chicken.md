intro: "This plate uses fully cooked, ready-to-eat sous-vide chicken; follow the package for storage and reheating. Warm the Danish and potatoes separately, then cook the eggs close to serving and assemble."
steps:
  - "Arrange 2 orange Danish pastries on a baking sheet and warm at 350°F (180°C) for 3–5 minutes; follow the package if it gives different directions. Put 2 tbsp honey mustard in a small bowl."
  - "Heat the 180 g fully cooked, ready-to-eat sous-vide chicken according to its package. If the package permits skillet reheating, warm it over medium heat with 1/2 tbsp olive oil, then slice and keep warm."
  - "In another skillet, heat 250 g pan-fried potatoes over medium heat until hot and golden. Keep warm."
  - "Use the remaining olive oil in a skillet to fry 2 eggs, covered, until both whites and yolks are set. For runny yolks, use pasteurized eggs."
  - "Arrange the Danish, chicken, potatoes, and fried eggs in separate sections on a large plate."
  - "Season the eggs with 1/3 tsp sea salt and 1/4 tsp black pepper. Spoon the honey mustard over the chicken and eggs, then serve."
tips:
  - The sous-vide chicken is already fully cooked. Follow its package for storage and reheating; searing the surface is not a sous-vide cooking step.
  - Cook ordinary eggs until both white and yolk are firm. Use pasteurized eggs if serving them runny, and keep the pastry separate until assembly.
storage: "Serve freshly made when possible. Refrigerate cooked leftovers within 2 hours, use within 3 to 4 days, and reheat to 165°F (74°C). Store opened sous-vide chicken as directed on its package. Keep the Danish separate from moist components and warm it again before serving."
