intro: "This is an untested home Benedict adaptation, not Second Floor Cafe’s original recipe. Layer toasted baguette with sautéed mushrooms, seared ham, and poached eggs, then finish with warm hollandaise."
steps:
  - Slice 2 baguette rolls crosswise; butter the cut sides and bake at 180°C /
    350°F 5–7 minutes until golden and lightly crisp.
  - Heat a skillet over medium with 1/2 tbsp olive oil and a little butter; add
    120 g sliced mushrooms; stir-fry until they release moisture, then reduce
    heat until dry; sprinkle 1/6 tsp sea salt and 1/8 tsp cracked black pepper;
    keep warm.
  - Add the listed 80 g ham to the same skillet; cook over medium-low until
    lightly browned and heated through; keep warm. Gently warm the listed 2 tbsp
    hollandaise over a double boiler or very low heat to avoid splitting.
  - Add water and 1 tbsp white vinegar to a saucepan and bring to a gentle
    simmer. Crack each egg into a small cup and poach one at a time for about
    3 minutes, until the white is set. Lift out and drain; repeat for all 4
    eggs. Use pasteurized eggs for runny yolks.
  - Cut each of the 2 baguette rolls into 2 pieces and divide the 4 pieces
    between 2 plates. Top each piece with sautéed mushrooms, ham, and 1 poached
    egg (4 eggs total).
  - Drizzle warm hollandaise to taste, then 1 tbsp olive oil; sprinkle 1/3 tsp
    sea salt and the remaining 1/8 tsp cracked black pepper.
  - Check the sauce coats evenly; serve hot while yolks run and bread stays warm.
tips:
  - "Warm the hollandaise gently in a water bath or over low heat so it does not separate; prepare the mushrooms and ham first, then poach the eggs just before assembling."
  - "Use pasteurized eggs for runny yolks; cook ordinary eggs until both whites and yolks are set."
storage: Serve freshly made when possible. Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours
