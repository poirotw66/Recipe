intro: Stir-fry the beef until just colored, then set aside and finish with the rice over high heat—grains stay separate and less likely to stick.
steps:
- Fluff 1 bowl of cooked rice with chopsticks to break up clumps; if using day-old cold rice, sprinkle 1–2 tsp water first. Slice 100 g beef against the grain into thin strips or small cubes. Chop 2 scallions, keeping whites and greens separate.
- In a small bowl, stir together 1.5 tbsp soy sauce and a pinch of salt; set aside.
- Heat a skillet over medium-high heat with 1 tbsp cooking oil. When hot, stir-fry the beef and scallion whites for 1–2 minutes until colored; transfer promptly to a plate.
- Add 1 tbsp cooking oil to the same pan. Toss in the rice over high heat and stir-fry for 2–3 minutes until grains are separate and lightly smoky.
- Return the beef and pour in the sauce mixture; stir-fry over high heat for 1 minute until everything is evenly coated.
- Scatter the scallion greens and toss for 10 seconds. Taste and add a pinch more salt if needed.
- Plate and serve while hot.
tips:
- High heat and quick hands give the best grain separation.
- Rest meat for 2–3 minutes before slicing; when pieces vary in thickness, check the thickest center for doneness.
storage: Best eaten right away; for bento, cook beef through fully.
