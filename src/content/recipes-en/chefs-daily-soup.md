intro: There is no fixed recipe—sweat onion for base flavor, then simmer harder vegetables.
steps:
- Chunk 1/2 carrot; cut 1/4 onion into fine strips; slice 100 g cabbage; chunk 1 tomato (score and blanch to peel if you like).
- "Heat 1 tsp olive oil in the pot over medium; stir-fry onion 1–2 minutes until translucent and fragrant."
- Add carrot and cabbage; stir-fry about 2 minutes until slightly softened and volume shrinks a little.
- "Pour in 800 ml water; bring to a boil over high heat, then cover and simmer on low about 12 minutes until root vegetables are tender."
- Uncover; add tomato and simmer 5 minutes more until tomatoes release juice and soften.
- Season with a pinch of salt and black pepper to taste and serve.
storage: Refrigerate up to 2 days; freeze up to 1 week; do not boil hard when reheating.
