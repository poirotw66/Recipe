intro: "Cook the ginger and vegetables first, then simmer the tomato juice, vodka, cream, and hot sauce as the base. Add the cooked rice last and work in hot water gradually."
steps:
  - "Prep: dice 100 g seasonal vegetables, julienne 5 g ginger, and loosen 220 g cooked jasmine rice. Measure the tomato juice, heavy cream, vodka, hot sauce, and spices from the ingredient list."
  - "Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a skillet over medium heat. Cook the ginger for about 30 seconds, then add the vegetables and stir-fry for 2 minutes."
  - "Add 2 tbsp tomato juice, 2 tbsp vodka, 30 ml heavy cream, and 1 tbsp hot sauce. Reserve a pinch each from the 1/4 tsp black pepper and 1/2 tsp chili flakes for plating; add the rest with 1/3 tsp sea salt. Simmer over low heat for 1 minute."
  - "Add the 220 g cooked rice and stir-fry for about 1 minute."
  - "Add hot water in 2 tbsp portions, 2–3 times, stirring until absorbed between additions; continue for about 4 minutes. The recipe does not specify a total amount, so adjust to the rice’s absorption."
  - "Stir to combine and taste. If needed, adjust the salt or heat using the listed seasonings."
  - "Plate in a deep dish and finish with the reserved black pepper and chili flakes."
  - "Serve hot."
tips:
  - "Julienne the listed 5 g ginger and measure the listed 30 ml cream and 1 tbsp hot sauce before cooking."
  - "Start with cooked, loosened rice. Add hot water in about 2 tbsp portions, 2–3 times, waiting for the rice to absorb each addition so it does not become too wet."
  - "Simmer the sauce for 1 minute before adding rice. Reserve a pinch of black pepper and chili flakes for the finish, as the method directs."
storage: "Serve soon after cooking. Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours and reheat to 165°F (74°C) before eating."
