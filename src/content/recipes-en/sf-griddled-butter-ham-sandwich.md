intro: "Butter and toast the cut sides of the rolls over low heat. Warm the ham, fry the eggs, and cook the wedges with the listed olive oil and seasonings; assemble the rolls with cheese, ham, and eggs."
steps:
  - "Split 2 bread rolls horizontally and spread butter as needed on the cut sides. Prepare 100 g ham, 40 g cheese, 2 raw eggs, and 250 g potato wedges."
  - "Heat a skillet over low and toast the cut sides of the rolls until golden; set aside."
  - "In the same skillet over medium heat, warm 100 g ham through and set aside."
  - "Melt a little butter in the skillet. Add 2 eggs, cover, and cook for 2–3 minutes. Cook ordinary eggs until both white and yolk are firm; use pasteurized eggs if serving runny."
  - "Toss 250 g potato wedges with 1 tbsp olive oil. Roast at 350°F (180°C) or pan-fry until golden; season with 1/3 tsp sea salt and 1/4 tsp black pepper."
  - "Layer 40 g cheese, the ham, and the eggs on the rolls; cover with the other half. If the cheese has not melted, return the sandwich to the skillet, cover, and warm over low heat until melted."
  - "Drizzle 2 tbsp maple syrup over the sandwich, serve with the wedges, and eat hot."
tips:
  - "Spread butter on the cut sides and toast over low heat; set the rolls aside before warming the ham in the same skillet."
  - "Cover the eggs and cook for 2–3 minutes. Cook ordinary eggs until both white and yolk are firm; use pasteurized eggs if serving them runny."
  - "Toss the wedges with the listed olive oil, sea salt, and black pepper before roasting or pan-frying. Add the maple syrup after assembling the sandwich."
storage: "Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours and reheat to 165°F (74°C). Store the bread and potato wedges separately."
