intro: "Dry the greens well. Sear the basa until the thickest part reaches 145°F (63°C); handle the fully cooked sous-vide beef according to its package, then arrange both proteins with the cheese-topped mushrooms over the greens."
steps:
  - "Heat 150 g fully cooked, ready-to-eat sous-vide beef according to its package directions. Cut 180 g basa into chunks, pat dry, and season with 1/3 tsp sea salt."
  - "Slice 120 g sautéed mushrooms. Heat half of the 1.5 tbsp olive oil in a skillet over medium heat, cook the mushrooms until hot and their moisture has evaporated, then set aside."
  - "Add the remaining olive oil to the skillet and cook the basa until done; check that the thickest piece reaches 145°F (63°C). Keep warm."
  - "Slice the beef according to its package directions; if it is already warm, slice and set aside."
  - "Shred or thinly slice 40 g cheese, place it over the hot mushrooms, and cover until melted."
  - "Wash and spin-dry 120 g mixed greens, then spread them over a large platter."
  - "Arrange the beef, basa, and cheesy mushrooms in separate sections over the greens and serve warm."
tips:
  - The sous-vide beef is fully cooked and ready to eat; follow its package for storage and reheating. Check the thickest part of the fish for 145°F (63°C).
  - Dry the greens thoroughly. Melt the cheese over the hot mushrooms before plating so the greens stay out of the hot pan.
storage: "Serve freshly made when possible. Refrigerate cooked leftovers within 2 hours and use within 3 to 4 days. Reheat cooked fish and mushrooms to 165°F (74°C); store opened sous-vide beef as directed on its package. Keep the lettuce separate from hot components."
