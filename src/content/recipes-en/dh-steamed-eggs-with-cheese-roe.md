intro: This is a home adaptation inspired by chawanmushi steaming, not a restaurant's original recipe. Two eggs are mixed with 200 ml cooled broth, steamed in two covered bowls, then topped with cheddar and ready-to-eat cooked flying fish roe.
steps:
- "Heat the broth according to its package or bring it briefly to a boil, then cool until warm but not hot. Gently beat the eggs, mix in the broth and a little salt, then strain through a fine-mesh sieve into two heatproof bowls. Skim visible foam with a spoon."
- "Heat the steamer water until steam rises, then lower the heat so the water simmers gently without rolling hard. Cover each bowl with a lid or heatproof plate; keep the water below the rims. Steam for about 12 minutes, then check. If needed, continue in 2-minute intervals. Once the custard is set, check the center and another spot with a food thermometer; both should reach at least 71.1°C / 160°F before serving."
- "Uncover and place one cheddar slice on each bowl. Cover for 1–2 minutes to soften it with residual heat; briefly steam again if it has not softened."
- "Top each bowl with about 15 g of cooked flying fish roe labeled ready to eat, a little sesame oil, and finely sliced scallion. Serve hot."
tips:
- Let the broth cool to warm or room temperature before adding the eggs; hot broth can partially set them.
- Cover the bowls and keep the heat gentle to limit drips and large holes. Bowl size and material, along with steamer heat, affect timing, so use the set center and temperature to judge doneness.
storage: Refrigerate leftovers in shallow containers at 4°C or below within 2 hours, or 1 hour above 32°C. Eat within 3 days and reheat to 74°C throughout.

This home adaptation uses two eggs and 200 ml broth. Heat the center to at least 160°F (71.1°C), and add cooked roe labeled ready to eat after steaming. It has not been kitchen-tested; steaming time, yield, texture, and cover-image consistency still need validation.
