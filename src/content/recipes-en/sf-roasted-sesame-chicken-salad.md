intro: Inspired by sesame chicken salads that pair cooked chicken with crisp

steps:
  - Do not rinse the chicken breast; pat dry. If uneven, butterfly or gently
    pound to an even thickness. Season both sides with salt and black pepper.
    Keep the board and knife used for raw chicken away from the salad
    vegetables.
  - Heat the oil in a skillet over medium heat. Cook the chicken for 5–7
    minutes, turn, and continue until the thickest center reaches 74°C / 165°F.
    Measure from the side; cook longer and recheck if needed.
  - Move the chicken to a clean board and rest for a few minutes before slicing.
    Wash and dry the greens, slice the cucumber, and halve the cherry tomatoes.
  - Toss the vegetables with the sesame dressing, plate, and top with chicken.
    Serve promptly.
tips:
  - Chicken thickness changes the cooking time; even thickness helps it cook
    uniformly. Keep raw and ready-to-eat foods separate, and dress the salad
    just before serving.
storage: Refrigerate the chicken and vegetables separately within 2 hours and
  use within 3 days. Keep the dressing separate.
