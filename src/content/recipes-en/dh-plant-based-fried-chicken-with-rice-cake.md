steps:
- If refrigerated rice cakes are firm, soften them in warm water according to the package and drain. Thaw the plant-based nuggets as directed
  and slice the onion.
- Mix gochujang, honey, garlic, and water in a small bowl.
- Heat the cooking oil in a skillet over medium heat. Cook the onion for 2 minutes until slightly translucent. Add the nuggets and heat through
  according to the package, lightly browning the surface.
- Add the rice cakes and sauce and stir for 1 minute. Cover and simmer over medium-low heat for 4–6 minutes, stirring every minute or two, until
  tender and coated. If the pan becomes dry, use up to 1 additional tablespoon from the listed 3 tablespoons of water.
- Top with sesame seeds, divide into two portions, and serve hot.
tips:
- Rice cakes vary in thickness and brand; cook until tender but chewy, and avoid a long high-heat simmer that can scorch the sauce.
storage: Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours and use within 1–2 days. Chilled rice cakes firm up; add a splash of water when reheating.
