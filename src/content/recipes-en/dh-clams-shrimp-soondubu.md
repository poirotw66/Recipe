steps:
  - "Prep: rinse clams 200 g and purge them according to the package directions;
    peel 6 shrimp leaving tails on and devein; slice ½ onion into strips and cut
    1 scallion into sections; portion 1 tbsp minced garlic, 1 tbsp soy sauce, 1
    tbsp Korean sesame oil, and a pinch of salt separately."
  - Preheat a Korean spicy stew pot or thick-bottomed small pot over medium heat
    for 1 minute. Add 1 tbsp Korean sesame oil, then sauté 1 tbsp minced garlic
    and onion strips until translucent and fragrant (about 2 minutes).
  - Add clams 200 g, cover, and steam over high heat 2 minutes until shells
    open. Uncover, add 6 shrimp, and stir-fry quickly until pink and curled
    (about 1 minute).
  - Pour in 400 ml broth and 1 tbsp soy sauce. Bring to a boil over high heat,
    then reduce to medium-low. Season with 1/4 tsp salt and simmer 5–8 minutes
    until the broth is sweet and all clams have opened; discard any that remain
    closed.
  - Lower the heat. Scoop 1 pack soft tofu in large chunks into the center of
    the pot. Gently push along the edge with a spoon so broth flows over the
    tofu without vigorous stirring (simmer 2 more minutes until piping hot).
  - Lower the heat to a gentle simmer, crack in 1 egg, cover, and cook for 3–5
    minutes until the white and yolk are fully set and the center reaches 71°C
    (160°F). Turn off the heat, then scatter scallions and sesame seeds over the
    stew.
