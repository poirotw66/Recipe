intro: Fried shallot crisps are the soul of the dish; reduce the braising liquid until thick so it clings to rice.
steps:
- Peel 2 shallots and slice thin. Place 1 bowl of cooked rice in a large bowl to keep warm.
- Heat a small saucepan over medium-high heat with 2 tbsp oil (or fat rendered from the pork). Fry the shallot slices until golden and lightly crisp to make fried shallot crisps; set most aside for garnish.
- In the same pot, add ground pork 150 g and stir-fry 3–4 minutes until the color changes, fat releases, and the meat breaks apart slightly.
- Add 3 tbsp soy sauce, 2 tbsp rice wine, 200 ml water, and 1 tsp rock sugar. Stir well and bring to a boil over high heat.
- Lower to a simmer, cover, and braise 12–15 minutes. Every 5 minutes, gently stir once to keep it from sticking to the bottom.
- Uncover and reduce over medium heat until the sauce is thick enough to coat a spoon. Taste and adjust salt and sweetness.
- Spoon the braised pork with sauce over the rice and top with the reserved fried shallot crisps.
storage: Refrigerate up to 3 days; add a little water when reheating.
