steps:
  - Clean the clams according to the package directions and discard any with
    cracked shells. Devein the shrimp and cut the squid into rings. Slice the
    onion, cut the scallion into sections, and cut the kimchi into roughly 3 cm
    pieces. Portion the seasonings and crack the egg into a small bowl.
  - Heat the sesame oil in a heavy pot over medium heat. Add the garlic and
    onion and sauté about 2 minutes until translucent.
  - Add the gochujang and stir over low heat for about 1 minute; do not let it
    scorch.
  - Add the broth and soy sauce and bring to a boil. Add the kimchi and squid,
    cover, and simmer gently for about 3 minutes until the squid is opaque.
  - Add the clams and shrimp and cover. Remove clams as soon as they open and
    discard any that remain closed; remove shrimp when the flesh is opaque to
    avoid overcooking.
  - Lower the heat. Scoop large pieces of soft tofu into the stew and gently
    move the broth around them without stirring vigorously. Heat about 2 minutes
    until piping hot.
  - Crack the egg into the center, cover, and simmer gently for about 3–5
    minutes. Use a food thermometer to confirm the egg center reaches 71°C
    (160°F) and both the white and yolk are fully set.
  - Return the clams and shrimp briefly to the pot until hot, scatter the
    scallion over the stew, and serve.
tips:
  - Remove clams as soon as their shells open and discard any that remain closed
    after cooking.
  - Cook shrimp until opaque, then remove it while the tofu and egg finish to
    avoid overcooking.
storage: Best served freshly cooked. Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours and
