intro: This home adaptation uses two thawed crust sheets and fresh peaches for one 20 cm double-crust pie. Cornstarch helps thicken the juices. Cool the pie completely for several hours so the filling can set before cutting six slices; this is not a restaurant's original recipe.
steps:
- Preheat the oven to 220°C. Peel and pit fresh peaches, slice them, and weigh 500 g after pitting. Mix 40 g sugar, 1 tsp cinnamon, and 20 g cornstarch first, then toss with the peaches until evenly coated.
- Thaw the crusts according to the package directions. Fit one sheet into a 20 cm pie dish without stretching it, leaving about 1 cm overhang. Add the peach filling. Cover with the second sheet, fold and press the edges together to seal, then cut 3–4 steam vents. Brush lightly with beaten egg and discard any unused raw egg.
- Set the pie on a parchment-lined baking sheet on the oven's lower rack. Bake at 220°C for 15 minutes, then reduce to 180°C and bake for about 35–45 minutes. The crust should be golden and the filling should bubble steadily through the vents or at the edge for at least 5 minutes before you remove it. Loosely shield the edge with foil if it browns early. Total baking time is about 50–60 minutes; actual time depends on the oven and pie dish.
- Set the pie on a rack to cool, then refrigerate it until fully cool and the filling has set; allow at least 2 hours total before cutting six slices. If saving the pie, refrigerate it within 2 hours of baking, or within 1 hour when the temperature is above 32°C / 90°F. Divide the optional 2 scoops of vanilla ice cream into small portions for serving. Crust thawing time is additional.
tips:
- A double crust traps more fruit juice, so keep the filling bubbling for at least 5 minutes and let the pie cool completely so it can set before slicing.
- Peach juiciness, pie-dish material, and oven temperature affect the result. The 20 cm pan, 20 g cornstarch, and bake time in this adaptation have not been kitchen-tested.
storage: "Store covered in the fridge for 3-4 days. Warm gently in a 160°C (320°F) oven for 5 minutes before serving with ice cream."
