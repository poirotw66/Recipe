steps:
  - Dice the chicken and finely chop the onion. Heat the oil in a skillet,
    soften the onion, then add the chicken and cook until its center reaches
    165°F (74°C); season with salt.
  - Spread chicken, cheese, and salsa over half of each tortilla and fold it
    closed.
  - Toast over medium-low heat for 2–3 minutes per side until golden and the
    cheese melts.
  - Cut into wedges and serve with avocado sauce and sour cream.
tips:
  - Do not overfill the tortillas; a thinner layer is easier to flip and heats
    evenly.
  - Cook the filling first, toast the bread or tortilla, and assemble shortly
    before serving so steam does not soften the exterior.
