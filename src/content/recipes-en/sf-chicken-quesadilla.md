intro: "Cook the chicken to 165°F (74°C), then place it with the cheese and salsa on half of each corn tortilla. Fold and griddle over medium-low heat for 2–3 minutes per side."
steps:
  - "Dice the chicken and finely chop the onion. Heat the cooking oil in a skillet, soften the onion, then add the chicken and cook until its center reaches 165°F (74°C); season with the listed 1/4 tsp salt."
  - "Divide the chicken between the 2 corn tortillas. Add 20 g cheese and 1 tbsp salsa to one half of each, then fold."
  - "Toast over medium-low heat for 2–3 minutes per side until golden and the cheese melts."
  - "Cut into wedges and serve with the avocado sauce and sour cream."
tips:
  - "Keep the filling on one half and leave room at the edge so the tortilla can fold and flip; cut the chicken small enough to check the thickest piece."
  - "Stay at medium-low heat for the listed 2–3 minutes per side. If the tortilla colors before the cheese melts, lower the heat and continue."
  - "The avocado sauce and sour cream are served alongside; keep them out of the filling."
storage: "Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours and reheat to 165°F (74°C). Store the avocado sauce and sour cream separately."
