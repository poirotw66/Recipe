steps:
  - Cut the chicken into bite-size pieces; dice the pepper and onion. Mix the
    curry powder, cumin, turmeric, salt, and pepper.
  - Heat the oil in a skillet and cook the chicken until the thickest pieces
    reach 165°F (74°C); transfer to a clean plate.
  - Cook the onion and pepper in the same skillet for 3 minutes. Add the cooked
    rice and half of the spice mix; stir-fry until hot throughout.
  - Return the chicken and remaining spices, toss to combine, and adjust
    seasoning. Serve in two portions.
