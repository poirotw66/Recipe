intro: Pound the single-serving pork loin chop to about 1 cm thick, marinate it with garlic, soy sauce, and rice wine, then air-fry. Timing varies with chop thickness and the appliance, so check the center with a food thermometer.
steps:
- Pound pork loin chop to about 1 cm thick with a meat mallet or the back of a knife; score tough membranes; pat surface dry with paper towels.
- Mince garlic (3 cloves); mix with soy sauce (1 tbsp), rice wine (1 tbsp), salt, black pepper, and olive oil (1 tsp) into a marinade. Rub both sides of the chop and marinate 10 minutes.
- While marinating, shred cabbage (80 g), massage with a pinch of salt for 3 minutes, rinse off salt, squeeze dry, and set aside.
- Preheat air fryer to 190°C for 3 minutes.
- Place chop in the basket in a single layer—do not fold or stack. Air-fry at 190°C for 8 minutes; open, flip, then air-fry 6 minutes more. Timing varies with chop thickness and the air fryer.
- Insert a food thermometer from the side into the thickest part of the chop. The center should reach 63°C (145°F); if it has not, air-fry for 2–3 more minutes and check again. Once it reaches that temperature, remove the chop and let it rest for at least 3 minutes before slicing.
- Slice and plate with shredded cabbage; serve hot.
storage: Best fresh; refrigerate up to 1 day; reheat at 160°C for 3 minutes.
