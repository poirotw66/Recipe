intro: "Cook the onion and 100 g sausage first. Divide the filling, cheese, and listed 2 tbsp salsa between two tortillas, fold, then griddle until the tortillas color and the cheese melts."
steps:
  - "Warm 2 tortillas. Thinly slice 100 g sausage, dice 1/2 onion, and shred 40 g cheese."
  - "Heat a little cooking oil in a skillet over medium heat. Cook the onion for 1 minute, add the 100 g sausage and cook according to the package directions until its center reaches 165°F (74°C). Season with 1/2 tsp sea salt and set aside."
  - "Heat another skillet over low. For each tortilla, put half the sausage-onion filling, 20 g cheese, and 1 tbsp salsa on one half."
  - "Fold each tortilla into a half-moon and press lightly with a spatula."
  - "Cook over medium-low for 2–3 minutes until the bottom has golden spots and the cheese edges start to melt."
  - "Flip and cook 2–3 minutes more until the tortilla is crisp and the cheese has melted."
  - "Cut into wedges and serve with 2 tbsp avocado sauce, 1.5 tbsp sour cream, and 1 tbsp barbecue sauce."
tips:
  - "Cook and set aside the sausage-onion filling, then divide it between the two tortilla halves; measure the salsa so the tortillas still fold closed."
  - "Cook over medium-low heat for 2–3 minutes per side, watching for golden spots and melting cheese at the edges. Lower the heat if the tortilla colors too quickly."
  - "The avocado sauce, sour cream, and barbecue sauce are served alongside; cut the cooked quesadilla into wedges before adding them."
