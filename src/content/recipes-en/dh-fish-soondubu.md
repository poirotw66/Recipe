intro: Sauté onion and garlic with gochujang, sear the sea bream, and simmer in broth until its
  thickest piece reaches 63°C (145°F). Add soft tofu, then cook the egg at a gentle simmer until the
  white and yolk are fully set and it reaches 71°C (160°F).
steps:
  - "Prep: Cut half an onion into strips and 1 scallion into sections; cut 150 g sea bream fillet
    into 3 cm pieces and pat dry with paper towels. Portion 1 tbsp minced garlic, 1.5 tbsp Korean
    chili paste (gochujang), 1 tbsp soy sauce, and 1 tbsp toasted sesame oil into small dishes.
    Crack 1 egg into a small bowl."
  - Heat a Korean stew pot or heavy-bottomed saucepan over medium heat. Add 1 tbsp toasted sesame
    oil, then sauté 1 tbsp minced garlic and the onion strips until the onion is translucent and
    fragrant, about 2 minutes.
  - Add 1.5 tbsp Korean chili paste (gochujang). Lower the heat and stir constantly with a spatula
    for about 1 minute until the chili oil aroma comes through—do not let it scorch.
  - Add the 150 g sea bream fillet and sear over medium heat for about 1 minute per side until the
    surface is opaque; turn it gently.
  - Pour in 400 ml kelp broth and 1 tbsp soy sauce. Bring to a boil, then reduce to medium-low,
    cover, and simmer 5–8 minutes. Use a food thermometer to confirm the thickest part of the fish
    reaches 63°C (145°F); continue cooking and recheck if needed.
  - Reduce to low heat. Scoop large spoonfuls of soft tofu into the center of the pot and gently
    push the broth along the sides so it flows through the tofu—avoid vigorous stirring. Simmer 2
    more minutes until piping hot.
  - Crack the prepared egg into the center of the stew. Cover and cook at a gentle simmer for about
    3–5 minutes; use a food thermometer to confirm the center of the egg reaches 71°C (160°F) and
    both the white and yolk are fully set before turning off the heat.
  - Scatter scallion sections and a pinch of sesame seeds over the top. Serve hot.
