  - "Cook fries 250 g according to package directions. If using frozen fries, keep them frozen and follow the package method; do not thaw or blot them first. Set aside truffle sauce 1 tbsp and grated cheese 15 g."
  - "While fries are hot, toss in a large bowl with sea salt 1/2 tsp."
  - "Add half of grated cheese 15 g and toss quickly so heat slightly melts the cheese."
  - "Drizzle truffle sauce 1 tbsp and toss gently; sprinkle remaining cheese on top."
  - "Mound high in a basket; serve while truffle aroma is strong and fries are still crisp."
