steps:
  - Heat the oven to 200°C / 400°F. Place the pork between sheets of wrap and
    gently pound to about 1 cm thick. Season; place the ham and cheese in the
    center with a border around the edges, then fold over and press the edges
    closed so the filling is enclosed.
  - Coat in flour, beaten egg, then breadcrumbs, covering the surface and seam
    with a thin layer. Set it on a clean tray. Heat oil in a skillet over medium
    heat and brown for about 2 minutes per side; the oven will finish cooking
    the center.
  - Transfer to a baking tray and bake about 10–15 minutes; actual time varies
    with cutlet thickness and the oven. Insert a thermometer from the side into
    the thickest pork, then check the center of the ham-and-cheese filling. This
    recipe uses 74°C / 165°F as a conservative target for both locations.
    Continue baking and recheck if needed.
  - Toast the baguette slices until lightly crisp. Mix mayonnaise with truffle
    paste, spread thinly on the bread, top with sliced pork, and serve hot.
tips:
  - Seal the edges and coat the seam to reduce cheese leakage. Truffle paste is
    potent, so mix in a small amount first. For a thin cutlet, insert the
    thermometer from the side and check the pork and filling separately.
storage: Refrigerate the cooked pork and bread separately within 2 hours and use
  within 3 days. Reheat until both the pork and filling centers reach 74°C /
  165°F.

This home-style open toast adapts the filled, breaded cutlet idea from pork cordon bleu; it is not a specific restaurant recipe. The 74°C / 165°F target is a conservative choice for this ham-and-cheese filling. Timing, yield, and texture have not been kitchen-tested.
