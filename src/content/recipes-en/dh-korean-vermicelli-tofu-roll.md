intro: Cook and drain the glass noodles, mix them with blanched vegetables and
  sautéed seasonings, then wrap the filling in tofu skin and pan-fry until
  golden.
steps:
  - Soak and cook 100 g Korean glass noodles according to the package directions
    until tender. Drain and cut into about 5 cm lengths. Blanch the spinach and
    carrot until tender, drain well, squeeze excess moisture from the spinach,
    and cut both into strips.
  - If needed, soften 4 fresh tofu-skin sheets according to their package
    directions. Dice half an onion and cut 1 scallion into sections.
  - Heat 1 tsp of the neutral oil in a skillet over low heat. Sauté 1 tbsp
    minced garlic and the onion for about 2 minutes until fragrant, then add the
    scallion and cook for about 1 minute until the onion softens.
  - Stir in 1 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp toasted sesame oil, and a pinch of salt,
    then turn off the heat. Fold in the cooked noodles, spinach, and carrot; let
    the filling cool slightly.
  - Lay a tofu-skin sheet flat, add a modest amount of filling, fold in the
    sides, and roll tightly. Place seam-side down. Repeat with the remaining
    sheets and filling.
  - Brush the skillet with the remaining 2 tsp neutral oil. Over medium heat,
    cook the rolls seam-side down until set, then turn each side until golden.
    Do not crowd the pan.
  - Serve hot. If slicing, let the rolls cool slightly and use a clean knife.
    Scallion or sesame may be added as an optional garnish.
tips:
  - Drain the noodles and vegetables well; excess moisture can tear the tofu
    skin.
  - Start seam-side down and let the seam set before turning. Use fresh tofu
    skin that is suitable for pan-frying.
storage: Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours. Reheat in a skillet until
  steaming hot; when reheating leftovers, bring the center to 74°C (165°F).

faqs:
  - question: Do the glass noodles need to be cooked first?
    answer: Yes. Cook them until tender according to the package directions, then
      drain and cut them. Soaking alone will not cook them during the brief
      pan-fry.
  - question: How do I keep the rolls from unwrapping?
    answer: Drain the filling, avoid overfilling, and cook the seam-side down first
      until it sets.
