steps:
  - Rinse 150 g uncooked white rice, add 180 ml water (about a 1:1.2 ratio),
    soak for 20 minutes, then cook until fully tender. Fluff and keep warm.
  - Slice duck jerky 1 pack thinly; shred cabbage 100 g and cut 1 scallion into
    short sections. Blanch cabbage 1 minute and drain; if jerky is very firm,
    steam 3 minutes before slicing.
  - Heat 1 tbsp Korean sesame oil in a skillet over medium heat. Stir-fry 100 g
    cabbage for about 2 minutes, add 1 pack duck jerky and 1 scallion, and
    stir-fry for 1 minute. Season with 1 tbsp soy sauce and 1/4 tsp salt; set
    aside.
  - Brush the stone pot evenly with 1 tbsp Korean sesame oil, spread hot rice
    and press flat, and arrange the stir-fried toppings neatly on top.
  - Place the stone pot over medium heat for about 4–6 minutes until a golden
    crust forms at the bottom and the rice smells toasted and sizzles; turn off
    the heat.
  - Before serving, mix rice and toppings together, or enjoy the crispy edge
    crust with duck jerky and vegetables for the best flavor.
