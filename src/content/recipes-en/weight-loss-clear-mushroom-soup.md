intro: Mushrooms bring umami—light seasoning; sip alongside a main or as a mini meal.
steps:
- Rinse 200 g mixed mushrooms quickly under running water—do not soak long or they absorb too much water.
- Trim tough stem ends; slice about 0.5 cm thick depending on thickness, drain on a plate, and set aside.
- Rinse 1 scallion; cut white part into thin segments and green part into rounds; keep separate.
- Add 700 ml water to a pot and bring to a boil over high heat; when bubbles form at the rim, reduce to medium heat.
- Add mushroom slices; once boiling again, lower to a simmer. Cover or leave uncovered—either works—and simmer gently 8–10 minutes until mushrooms soften and release umami.
- Uncover and season with a pinch of salt and white pepper; taste—the broth should stay light so mushroom flavor stays forward.
- Turn off heat, scatter scallion greens, stir gently, and serve hot.
storage: Refrigerate 2 days; do not boil hard on reheat.
