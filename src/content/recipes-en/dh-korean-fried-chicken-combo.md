intro: Coat and fry the chicken to a safe internal temperature, then divide it between a spicy sauce
  and a honey-soy glaze.
steps:
  - Cut the onion into thin strips. Cut the chicken thigh into about 3 cm pieces, pat dry, and keep
    raw-chicken tools and surfaces separate from ready-to-eat food.
  - Season the chicken with salt, black pepper, and sesame oil. Cover and refrigerate for 30 minutes
    while preparing the sauce ingredients.
  - Spread the Korean fried chicken mix in a shallow dish. Coat the chicken in batches and shake off
    excess; keep flour and utensils that touched raw chicken away from cooked food.
  - Add the neutral oil to an 18 cm heavy-bottomed pot, keeping the oil below halfway up the pot.
    Heat to 170°C using an oil thermometer. Carefully add chicken in batches and fry about 6–8
    minutes. Use a food thermometer to confirm the center of the largest piece reaches 74°C (165°F);
    continue cooking and recheck if needed.
  - Lift the chicken out with a spider and drain on a rack for about 3 minutes. Bring the oil back
    to 185°C and fry in batches for another 1–2 minutes until golden and crisp.
  - In a small saucepan, heat 1 tsp sesame oil over low heat and soften half the onion. Stir in the
    gochujang, half the honey, 1 tsp soy sauce, and 1 tbsp water until slightly thickened, then turn
    off the heat.
  - Wash the saucepan, then heat the remaining 1 tsp sesame oil and soften the remaining onion with
    the garlic. Add the remaining honey, 2 tsp soy sauce, and 1 tbsp water; simmer until slightly
    thickened and turn off the heat.
  - Divide the chicken between two clean bowls and toss each half with one sauce. Optionally garnish
    with sesame seeds and scallion; serve hot.
tips:
  - Use a food thermometer to confirm the center of the largest chicken piece reaches 74°C (165°F);
    a golden coating alone does not show that the inside is cooked.
  - Keep the pot less than half full of oil, dry the chicken well, and fry in batches to reduce
    splattering and temperature drops.
storage: Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours and reheat to 74°C (165°F). The chicken is crispiest
  when freshly fried.
