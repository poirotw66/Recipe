intro: Gently set 180 g egg whites over low heat, then briefly stir-fry with half a cucumber to keep the curds tender and the cucumber crisp.
steps:
- Pour 180 g egg whites into a bowl, add a pinch of salt, and whisk until fine bubbles appear. Rinse and dry 1/2 cucumber, then cut it into roughly 0.5 cm cubes.
- Heat a skillet over low and add 1 tsp olive oil; when oil is just warm, pour in whites—do not wait for heavy smoke (low heat keeps them tender).
- After pouring, let sit about 10 seconds so the bottom sets slightly, then use a spatula to push gently from the outer edge toward the center; about 1 minute to form large soft curds (better texture than scrambling into fine bits).
- Turn heat to high, add diced cucumber, and stir-fry about 1 minute until bright green and still slightly crisp.
- Sprinkle a pinch of white pepper, fold quickly 10 seconds, taste, and add a little more salt if needed.
- Once the egg whites are fully set with no liquid center, transfer immediately to a plate; serve with rice or sweet potato if desired.
storage: Best eaten immediately. Cool any leftovers promptly, refrigerate in a sealed container, eat within 1 day, and reheat until steaming hot throughout.
faqs:
- question: Can I use liquid egg whites?
  answer: Yes. Weigh out 180 g pasteurized liquid egg whites. If separating whole eggs, seal and refrigerate the yolks immediately and use them promptly in a thoroughly cooked dish.
- question: Why does cucumber egg white stir-fry turn watery?
  answer: The cucumber may not have been dried or the whites may have cooked too long. Stir-fry the cucumber briefly over high heat and plate as soon as the whites set.
