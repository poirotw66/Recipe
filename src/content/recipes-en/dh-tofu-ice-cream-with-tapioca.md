intro: Blend silken tofu, cream, and brown sugar, then freeze with periodic stirring. Serve with quick-cooking tapioca pearls; allow at least four hours of freezing.
steps:
- Drain and chill 300 g refrigerated silken tofu labeled ready to eat. Blend it with 100 ml cream and 30 g brown sugar until smooth, then transfer to a shallow freezer-safe container.
- Freeze. For the first 2 hours, remove and stir thoroughly every 30 minutes. Continue freezing for at least 4 hours total, until firm in the center; a home freezer may take longer.
- Cook the listed 50 g of quick-cooking brown-sugar tapioca pearls according to their package. In a small pan, warm the remaining 20 g brown sugar with 30 ml water until dissolved; cool to make syrup.
- Scoop the ice cream with a clean utensil, drizzle with a little syrup, and top with the pearls just before serving.
tips:
- Choose refrigerated tofu labeled ready to eat; keep chilled and follow the use-by date.
- A shallow container freezes more evenly. Scrape the frozen edges into the center each time you stir.
storage: Use pasteurized cream and refrigerated tofu labeled ready to eat; keep both chilled
  as directed on their packages and limit time at room temperature. Keep the ice cream sealed
  in the freezer. Hand-stirred ice cream has a different texture from churned ice cream, so serve
  it soon after freezing; storage quality for this recipe has not been tested. Cook pearls separately
  and refrigerate leftovers according to the package.
