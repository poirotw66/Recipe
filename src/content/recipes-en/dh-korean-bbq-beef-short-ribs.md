intro: "Marinate thin flat-iron beef in Korean barbecue sauce in the

steps:
  - Cut 200 g flat-iron beef across the grain into slices about 0.5 cm thick.
    Slice half an onion and cut 1 scallion into sections.
  - Mix 3 tbsp Korean barbecue sauce, 1 tbsp sesame oil, and 1 tbsp minced
    garlic. Coat the beef and refrigerate for 15–30 minutes.
  - Preheat a griddle or heavy skillet over medium-high heat. Add 1 tsp sesame
    oil and coat the surface. Sear the beef in uncrowded single layers.
  - Cook each side for about 1–2 minutes until browned. Use a food thermometer
    to confirm the beef reaches 63°C (145°F); continue cooking and recheck if
    needed. Transfer to a clean plate and rest for 3 minutes.
  - Cook the onion in the same pan for 1–2 minutes until softened. Return the
    beef and juices briefly to the pan until hot.
  - Scatter scallion over the beef and serve hot. Rice or lettuce may be served
    alongside but is not included in the ingredient list.
tips:
  - Sear the thin beef in batches so the pan stays hot and the meat browns
    instead of steaming.
  - After the beef reaches 63°C (145°F), rest it for at least 3 minutes; do not
    use browning or “juiciness” as a doneness test.
storage: Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours and reheat to 74°C (165°F).
