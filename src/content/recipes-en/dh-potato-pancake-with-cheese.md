steps:
- Peel and shred the potatoes. Soak for 5 minutes, drain, and pat dry. Dice the ham, sausage, and bell pepper; drain the corn.
- Whisk flour, egg, cold water, and salt into a thick batter. Fold in the potatoes and remaining add-ins.
- Heat half the cooking oil in a skillet over medium heat. Spread half the batter into an 18 cm round, lower to medium-low, cover, and cook 4–5
  minutes until the underside is set.
- Flip and cook 3–4 minutes until the center is cooked through. Sprinkle with half the cheese, cover, and cook about 1 minute until melted. Keep
  warm, then repeat with the remaining oil and batter.
- Cut into wedges and serve hot. Prepare a dipping sauce separately if desired.
tips:
- Drain the potatoes well or the batter will thin. Cook one pancake at a time so it is easier to flip and cook through.
