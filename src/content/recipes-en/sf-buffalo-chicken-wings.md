intro: Inspired by oven-baked Buffalo wings and their butter-hot-sauce coating,

steps:
  - Heat the oven to 230°C / 450°F. Do not rinse the wings; pat very dry and
    toss with oil, salt, and black pepper. Arrange in one layer with space on a
    wire rack set over a baking tray.
  - Bake for 20 minutes, turn, then bake another 15–20 minutes. Insert a
    thermometer from the side into the thickest meat of the largest wing; it
    must reach 74°C / 165°F. Continue baking and recheck if needed.
  - Melt the butter in a small pan over low heat. Stir in minced garlic and hot
    sauce; do not boil.
  - Once the wings reach 74°C / 165°F, transfer them to a clean bowl, toss with
    the sauce, and serve hot.
tips:
  - Wing size and oven performance affect timing. Leave space on the tray and
    check the thickest meat on the largest wing.
storage: Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours and use within 3 days. Reheat
  wings to 74°C / 165°F.
