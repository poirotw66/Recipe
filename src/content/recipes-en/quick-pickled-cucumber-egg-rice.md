intro: Preserved radish is salty—rinse 5 minutes and go easy on extra salt.
steps:
- "Dice 2 tbsp preserved radish (cai po), soak in water 5 minutes, drain, and taste for salt; beat 2 eggs; mince 1 garlic clove; mound 1 bowl rice in a serving bowl."
- "Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a skillet over medium heat; sauté garlic 30 seconds until fragrant."
- "Add preserved radish and stir-fry over medium heat 1–2 minutes until aromatic (do not let it scorch)."
- "Pour beaten egg down the pan edge; lower to medium-low and gently push from the edges toward the center into soft curds (about 1–2 minutes)."
- "Turn off heat when eggs are about 70% set (residual heat will finish them); taste—cai po is usually salty enough without extra salt."
- Spoon cai po egg and juices over rice; optional scallions or white pepper on top.
storage: Best fresh; microwave reheat about 1 minute.
