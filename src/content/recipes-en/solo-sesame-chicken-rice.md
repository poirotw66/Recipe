intro: Marinate strips 10 minutes before a quick stir-fry; sprinkle sesame at the end to keep aroma.
steps:
- Cut chicken breast 180 g into strips against the grain. Mince 2 cloves garlic. Toss the chicken with 1 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp rice wine, and 1/2 tsp sugar; marinate 10 minutes.
- Place 1 bowl of cooked rice in a large bowl to keep warm.
- Heat a skillet over medium-high heat with 1 tbsp cooking oil. When the oil is hot, add the minced garlic and stir-fry 20 seconds until fragrant.
- Add the chicken strips in a single layer. Pan-fry 2–3 minutes until the surface turns white, flip, and keep cooking until the center is done (about 2 more minutes).
- Add the remaining 1 tbsp soy sauce and 2–3 tbsp water. Stir-fry over high heat for 1 minute until the sauce thickens and coats the chicken.
- Turn off the heat, sprinkle 1 tbsp white sesame seeds, and toss. Taste and adjust seasoning.
- Spoon the sesame chicken with sauce over the rice and serve.
storage: Refrigerate 1 day; microwave covered with a damp paper towel about 1 minute to reheat.
