intro: Press out water for a meaty bite; glaze after both sides are golden.
steps:
- Cut 1 pack firm tofu into about 1.5 cm thick slabs (3–4 pieces); layer paper towels above and below, place a plate or cutting board on top with weight, and press 15 minutes to drain; change towels and pat surfaces completely dry (the more water removed, the meatier the sear).
- Mix 1.5 tbsp soy sauce and 1 tbsp mirin in a bowl for teriyaki glaze; set 1 tsp white sesame seeds beside it. Break 100 g broccoli into small florets, rinse, and drain.
- Add about 400 ml water to a skillet and bring to a boil over high heat; blanch broccoli 2 minutes until bright green and crisp; drain and set aside. Pour out water and wipe pan dry.
- Heat skillet over medium-high and add 1 tsp olive oil; when hot, lay tofu slabs in a single layer and sear first side about 3 minutes until golden.
- Flip carefully and sear 3 more minutes until both sides are golden, outside slightly firm, inside still tender (use two spatulas to flip and avoid breaking).
- Turn heat to low, drizzle teriyaki glaze along pan edge, and swirl pan so slabs absorb sauce evenly; simmer about 1–2 minutes until glaze thickens slightly and tofu looks glossy (flip once if needed to coat both sides).
- Plate tofu steaks with blanched broccoli on the side, sprinkle 1 tsp white sesame seeds, drizzle remaining pan sauce over tofu, and serve hot.
storage: Refrigerate up to 2 days; re-crisp in a skillet 1 minute.
