intro: "Toast the rolls and cook the mushrooms first. Drain the capers, prepare the salmon, then poach the eggs and make the hollandaise before assembling."
steps:
  - "Slice 2 baguette rolls crosswise; spread the listed butter for the rolls on the cut sides and bake at 180°C / 350°F 5–7 minutes until golden and lightly crisp."
  - "Heat a skillet over medium with 1 tbsp olive oil; add 120 g mushrooms; stir-fry until they release moisture, then reduce heat until dry; sprinkle 1/6 tsp sea salt; keep warm."
  - "Keep 120 g smoked salmon chilled until just before assembly; roll into bite-size portions and drain 1 tbsp capers."
  - "Bring a saucepan of water to a boil with 1 tbsp white vinegar; reduce heat; stir a gentle whirlpool with a spoon; crack in 2 eggs and poach over low heat about 3 minutes until whites set and yolks runny; drain well."
  - "Make the hollandaise: whisk 2 egg yolks with 1 1/2 tsp lemon juice until pale and thick. Set the bowl over barely simmering water without letting it touch the water; whisk rapidly. Remove from the heat and slowly stream in 57 g melted, warm (not hot) unsalted butter while whisking until smooth and thick enough to leave a brief trail. Season with a pinch of salt and cayenne and keep warm. Use pasteurized egg yolks because the sauce is not fully cooked."
  - "Divide toasted baguette on plates; layer sautéed mushrooms, smoked salmon, and 2 poached eggs on each; garnish with 1 tbsp capers."
  - "Spoon warm hollandaise over the stack; sprinkle 1/6 tsp sea salt and 1/4 tsp cracked black pepper; serve hot."
tips:
  - "Drain the measured tablespoon of capers, and reduce the mushrooms’ released moisture before holding them warm."
