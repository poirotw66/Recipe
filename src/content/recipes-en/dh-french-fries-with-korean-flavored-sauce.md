intro: Double-fry the potatoes and serve them with a sweet-spicy dip. Drying the potatoes and
  checking the oil temperature helps limit splattering and promotes even browning.
steps:
  - Cut 200 g potatoes into sticks about 1 cm thick. Soak in cold water for 10 minutes, drain, and
    pat completely dry with paper towels.
  - Mix 1.5 tbsp gochujang, 1 tbsp honey, and a pinch of salt in a small bowl; set aside as a dip.
  - Add enough neutral oil to a heavy pot and heat to 160°C (320°F), checking with a food
    thermometer. Fry the potatoes in batches without crowding.
  - Fry for about 5 minutes until softened and pale golden. Drain on a wire rack and rest for 5
    minutes.
  - Raise the oil to 190°C (375°F). Fry the potatoes in batches for about 2 minutes until crisp and
    golden, then drain.
  - Season lightly with salt while hot. Serve the dip on the side so the fries stay crisp.
  - Serve immediately. Chopped scallion or sesame is optional and is not required by this recipe.
tips:
  - Dry the potatoes thoroughly and fry in batches; wet surfaces and crowded oil increase
    splattering and reduce crispness.
  - Taste the dip before serving and adjust only if desired; keep it separate until eating.
storage: Fries are best fresh. Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours and reheat in an oven or air
  fryer to 74°C (165°F).

faqs:
  - question: How can I keep the fries crisp?
    answer: Dry the potatoes, fry in batches, and keep the dip separate. Reheating in an oven is more
      likely to restore crispness than microwaving.
  - question: Can I skip the second fry?
    answer: Yes, but the fries will be less crisp. If skipping it, make sure the potatoes are fully
      cooked and golden.
