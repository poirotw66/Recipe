intro: Season a fully thawed half chicken and roast it through, brushing with a
  little BBQ sauce near the end. Do not rinse raw poultry; measure the breast
  and thigh separately.
steps:
  - If frozen, thaw the half chicken completely in the refrigerator. Do not
    rinse raw poultry. Heat the oven to 200°C / 400°F. Pat the skin dry and rub
    with olive oil, salt, pepper, and paprika; place skin-side up in a roasting
    tray.
  - Cut the potatoes into chunks and add them around the chicken after about 20
    minutes. Roast the chicken for about 45–60 minutes more, adjusting for its
    size.
  - Insert a thermometer from the side into the thickest breast and thigh
    sections; both must reach 74°C / 165°F. Continue roasting and recheck every
    few minutes if needed. Brush on the BBQ sauce during only the final 10
    minutes to reduce scorching.
  - Cut the corn into sections and add it for the final 20 minutes until hot.
    Once the chicken reaches temperature, transfer it to a clean plate and rest
    for 5 minutes before carving. Serve with the potatoes and corn.
tips:
  - Do not use clear juices or a fixed roasting time as a substitute for
    temperature. Both the thickest breast and thigh sections must reach 74°C /
    165°F. Do not wash raw chicken; clean and sanitize surfaces and tools after
    contact.
storage: Refrigerate cooked chicken and sides in separate containers within 2
  hours and use within 3 days. Reheat chicken to 74°C / 165°F.
